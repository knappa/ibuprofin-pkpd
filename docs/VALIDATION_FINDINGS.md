# Validation Findings

Interpretation of `docs/VALIDATION.md` (generated by
`node scripts/validation.mjs`). No parameters were adjusted in response to
these results.

## Summary

Of the scored independent comparisons: 23 PASS, 3 ACCEPTABLE, 1 FAIL
(plus 4 INFO rows that are shown but not scored).
The FAIL and the systematic patterns below are documented rather than tuned
away.

## 1. Pediatric PK and PD timing: good agreement

Across nahata1991, kelley1992 and kauffman1992 (5-10 mg/kg suspension,
children 11 months to 11.5 years), the allometrically scaled adult model
reproduces Cmax, Tmax, oral clearance, half-life and AUC within the
reported spread. The adult-derived suspension absorption half-life
(19.2 min) is close to the pediatric estimates (16.3 min, kelley1992;
0.3 h, kauffman1992). The antipyresis timing also agrees: temperature nadir
at 2.6 h (kelley1992 observed 3.05 h) and a 2.0 h delay from peak
concentration to peak temperature decrement (kauffman1992: 1-3 h).

## 2. Adult Cmax is systematically about 20-25% low

| comparison | predicted | observed |
|---|---|---|
| li2012, 400 mg tablet | 23.5 mg/L | 30.7 +/- 8.6 |
| blain2002, 400 mg fed, at 2.5 h | 18.5 mg/L | 24 +/- 8 |
| nahata1991, 5 mg/kg (child) | 20.9 mg/L | 28.4 +/- 7.5 |
| morse2022 Table 4 (the source's own simulations) | 17.6 mg/L | median 24.1 |

Each is within about 1 SD of the observed mean, but the direction is
consistent. Oral clearance is
also slightly high relative to li2012 (4.03 vs 3.66 L/h) and kelley1992.
Possible causes, not yet resolved:

- A typical-value prediction versus a population mean. For log-normal
  variability the mean exceeds the typical value. But a Monte Carlo run with
  morse2022's between-subject variability (Table 3) leaves the median Cmax
  at 17.5 mg/L (typical value 17.6); adding their residual error at their
  simulation sampling times raises it only to 20.6 mg/L, not 24.1.
- morse2022's Table 4 simulations do not follow from its Table 3 as
  published: the ratio of their Cmax to ours is 1.22-1.37 across all six
  formulation/feeding combinations, and their fed Tmax values are shorter
  than the Table 3 lag-time factors imply. A transcription error in
  Table 3 (for example V1 or an absorption parameter) cannot be ruled out.
- Scaling by total body weight instead of normal fat mass. This is exact for
  the reference 70 kg male, so it cannot explain the gap for typical
  subjects.

**Status:** accepted for v1 (project owner decision, 2026-09-18) and stated
on the page: peak concentrations may be underestimated by about 20%.
REVIEW-LATER (adult-cmax-bias): ask the morse2022 authors about Table 3
versus Table 4, or cross-check against a second adult population PK model.

## 3. COX-1 inhibition after a single dose: FAIL

Predicted 83% at 2.5 h versus observed 96 +/- 3% (blain2002, same
volunteers as the in vitro curve). Two contributors:

- The predicted plasma concentration is lower than observed (item 2).
  At the observed 24 mg/L the model gives 85%.
- The Hill slope (0.616) is derived from the mean-curve IC50/IC80 assuming
  0-100% plateaus. Averaging curves across subjects flattens the slope. The
  mean of individual IC50/IC80 values (Table 1 column a) gives a steeper
  slope (0.93) but a higher IC50 (2.06 mg/L), and still predicts 88%
  (91% at the observed concentration).

Observed COX-1 inhibition is near its ceiling with a small SD (3%), so any
shallow curve fails this test. **Status:** known limitation. The page should
present COX-1 inhibition as approximate and note that measured inhibition
after 400 mg is about 90-96%.

COX-2 (predicted 76%, observed 83 +/- 12%) passes, even though blain2002
reports that its in vitro curve underestimates ex vivo COX-2 inhibition.

## 4. Not scored (INFO)

- **blain2002 repeated dosing:** predicted 18.9 / 26.9 / 20.7 mg/L at 2.5 h
  after the 09:00 / 12:00 / 20:00 doses on day 3, versus 14.8 +/- 5.9. The
  observed value fell from 24.0 (single dose) to 14.8 (repeated), which no
  linear model can reproduce; which dose was sampled is not stated.
- **brown1998 dose proportionality:** a 2-fold dose gave a 1.5-fold AUC;
  the linear model gives 2.0. nahata1991 found no dose effect over the same
  range. Known limitation.
- **anderson2019 steady-state effect-site trough** (400 mg every 8 h):
  predicted 5.5 mg/L versus their model-based 6.3 mg/L. Consistent.
